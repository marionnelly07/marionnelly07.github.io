About me

I am a PhD candidate in Political Science at Sciences Po’s Centre for International Studies (CERI), supervised by Carola Klöck. I am drawn to the actors that International Relations often treats as footnotes: non-sovereign territories that sit inside centralised states yet keep finding ways to engage with the world around them.

My doctoral research follows Reunion Island, a French overseas territory in the southwestern Indian Ocean. I ask how issue framing works as a partial, negotiated substitute for the recognition, legitimacy, and access that formal sovereignty would otherwise confer, where security, development, and the environment meet.

I hold an MRes in Political Science (International Relations) from Sciences Po (2025). Before starting the PhD, I completed a research internship on crisis governance at the Université de La Réunion and carried out preliminary fieldwork in Reunion Island and Mauritius.
